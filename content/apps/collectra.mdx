# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Collectra** ("Collectra," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Collectra mobile application.

Collectra is provided by **Kyaw Zayar Tun**.

- Website: [https://kyawzayartun.com](https://kyawzayartun.com)
- Contact page: [https://kyawzayartun.com/contact](https://kyawzayartun.com/contact)
- Email: [kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com](mailto:kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com)

## 1. Overview

Collectra is an offline-first application for organizing personal collections.

The current version does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization. Collection information you create is stored locally on your device unless you intentionally export or share it.

Collectra does not sell personal information, display third-party advertising, or track you across applications or websites owned by other companies.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally:

- Collection names and descriptions
- Item names, descriptions, notes, tags, barcodes, and metadata
- Purchase prices, estimated values, and price history
- Loan records
- Favorite and wishlist selections
- Photos you select or capture
- App preferences, including language, theme, view settings, and consent choices
- Notification preferences
- Locally generated import and export data
- Cached metadata and images

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly use an export, share, backup, or other system feature that sends it to a destination you select.

We cannot access locally stored collection data remotely.

## 3. Accounts and Cloud Synchronization

The current public version of Collectra does not offer user accounts or cloud synchronization.

Because no Collectra account is created, we do not collect account registration information such as your name, email address, or password, and there is no server-side Collectra account to delete.

If account or cloud synchronization features are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are made available.

## 4. Information Processed Automatically

Collectra uses certain Firebase services to understand app reliability, performance, and feature usage.

### 4.1 Firebase Analytics

With your consent, Collectra may use Firebase Analytics to process information such as:

- App launches and sessions
- Screens or features used
- General interaction events
- App version
- Device model and operating-system version
- Language and general region
- Firebase installation or vendor identifiers
- General technical information about the App

We do not intentionally include collection names, item names, notes, photos, barcodes, prices, loan details, or other user-created collection content in analytics events.

Analytics collection can be accepted, declined, or disabled from the App's settings. Collectra does not use Firebase Analytics for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

### 4.2 Firebase Crashlytics

Collectra uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and improve stability. Crash reports may include:

- Crash stack traces and error information
- App version and bundle identifier
- Device model, architecture, and operating-system version
- Available memory and storage information
- App state at the time of a crash
- Crashlytics and Firebase installation identifiers
- Time and technical context of the crash

We do not intentionally attach collection content, photos, notes, barcodes, or other user-created data to crash reports.

According to Firebase's published service information, Crashlytics generally retains crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.

### 4.3 Firebase Performance Monitoring

Collectra may use Firebase Performance Monitoring to measure application startup time, screen performance, and network reliability. This may include:

- Firebase installation and session identifiers
- Device model, operating system, language, and app version
- General network type and country derived from an IP address
- Performance timings
- Network request URLs without request parameters or payload contents
- Response codes, response times, and payload sizes

Firebase states that IP-associated performance events are generally retained for 30 days and installation-associated or de-identified performance data for 60 days before removal begins.

### 4.4 Firebase Remote Config

Collectra uses Firebase Remote Config to receive configuration values, such as whether a feature is available. Firebase may process technical service information, including a Firebase installation identifier, IP address, app version, and configuration-request information.

Remote Config does not receive the contents of your collections.

### 4.5 Firebase Cloud Messaging

If push notifications are made available and you choose to enable them, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process:

- A Firebase installation identifier
- A push-notification token
- App and device information needed to deliver notifications
- Notification delivery information

You can decline notification permission or disable notifications through Collectra's settings or your device settings.

## 5. Metadata Search Services

Collectra can retrieve public information about books, movies, television programs, and video games from third-party metadata providers.

Depending on the type of item you search for, Collectra may contact:

- Google Books
- The Movie Database ("TMDB")
- Internet Game Database ("IGDB")
- Twitch authentication services used by IGDB

When you perform a metadata search or barcode lookup, the App may send the relevant search term, title, ISBN, barcode, or public item identifier to the appropriate provider.

These providers may also receive technical information normally transmitted during an internet request, such as your IP address, request time, device network information, and request headers.

Collectra does not send your complete collection database, notes, loan records, prices, or unrelated photos to metadata providers. Images supplied by metadata services, such as book covers, movie posters, or game artwork, may be downloaded directly from the provider's image servers.

Use of these services is subject to their respective policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Books API documentation](https://developers.google.com/books)
- [TMDB Privacy Policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy)
- [TMDB API Terms of Use](https://www.themoviedb.org/api-terms-of-use)
- [Twitch Privacy Notice](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/privacy-notice/)
- [IGDB API documentation](https://api-docs.igdb.com/)

## 6. Camera, Photos, and Files

Collectra may request access to:

### Camera

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a barcode or capture a photo for a collection item.

### Photo library

Photo-library access is used when you choose to select or save an image.

### Files

File access is used when you choose to import collection data or export a backup.

Collectra only requests these permissions when needed for the relevant feature. You can deny or revoke access through your device settings. Denying a permission prevents the related feature from working but does not prevent you from using unrelated parts of the App.

## 7. Exports and Sharing

When you export or share information, Collectra creates a file containing the data you selected and passes it to the operating system's file picker or share interface.

The destination application or service you choose may receive and process that exported file according to its own privacy policy. We do not control how a third-party destination handles information you intentionally send to it.

Exported files remain under your control and may continue to exist until you delete them from your device, cloud-storage provider, messaging application, or other selected destination.

## 8. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App and its service providers is used to:

- Provide collection-management features
- Retrieve requested public metadata
- Save your preferences
- Diagnose crashes and technical problems
- Measure and improve performance
- Understand feature usage when you consent to analytics
- Deliver notifications when enabled
- Prevent abuse and maintain service security
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles or sell it to data brokers.

## 9. Data Sharing

We may share limited technical information with service providers only as necessary to provide the functions described in this policy.

These providers include Google Firebase and the metadata providers listed above.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request
- Protect the rights, safety, and security of users or others
- Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
- Complete a business transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards

We do not sell or rent personal information.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

### Local data

Collection data and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you:

- Delete individual items or collections
- Clear the App's data
- Delete exported files
- Uninstall the App

### Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase retains analytics, crash, performance, installation, and service information according to the applicable Firebase or Google settings and retention policies.

Current Firebase service-retention information is available at:

[Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Deletion requests

Because the current version does not create a Collectra account or upload your collection database to a Collectra backend, we generally cannot identify or remotely delete data stored only on your device.

For questions or requests involving information processed through our Firebase project, contact us at [kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com](mailto:kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com). We may need information from you to verify and process a request.

## 11. Your Choices

You can:

- Use the core App without creating an account
- Accept or decline analytics collection
- Disable analytics in the App's settings
- Decline or revoke camera, photo, file, and notification permissions
- Delete local collection information
- Delete exported files
- Uninstall the App
- Contact us regarding privacy questions or applicable data rights

## 12. International Data Processing

Firebase and metadata providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

These countries may have different data-protection laws. Service providers process information according to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and legal obligations.

## 13. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

Local collection information is protected by the security controls provided by your device and operating system. We recommend using a device passcode, biometric protection where available, current operating-system updates, and secure handling of exported files.

No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 14. Children's Privacy

Collectra is not directed specifically to children under 13 or the minimum applicable age in their country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account system because the current version does not provide accounts. Firebase services may process limited technical information as described in this policy.

If you believe information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately, contact us at [kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com](mailto:kyawzayartun.contact@gmail.com).
