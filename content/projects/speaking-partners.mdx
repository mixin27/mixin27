An Android application that helps language learners find a person to practise speaking with.

## Experience

- Matches learners for one-to-one speaking practice.
- Uses Firebase for account and real-time application data.
- Integrates Agora to support live voice conversations.

## Focus

The project explores a lightweight, welcoming flow for moving from discovery to a real conversation without making learners manage complex setup.
