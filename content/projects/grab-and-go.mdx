Grab & Go is a branch management app for convenience-store operations.

## Scope

- Gives branch teams a dedicated mobile workspace for operational tasks.
- Organizes store-level information in a consistent, easy-to-navigate interface.
- Supports a practical workflow designed for day-to-day branch management.

## Technology

Built with Flutter, Riverpod, AutoRoute, and Drift for a responsive mobile experience with structured local data handling.
