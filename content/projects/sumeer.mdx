Sumeer is a mobile CV and resume builder for creating a professional document without starting from a blank page.

## Key capabilities

- Collects personal, education, experience, and skills information in guided sections.
- Lets users choose a layout and refine the content before exporting.
- Generates a shareable PDF resume directly from the app.

## Technology

Flutter provides the cross-platform interface, with Riverpod for state management, AutoRoute for navigation, Firebase-backed services, and PDF generation for the final document.
