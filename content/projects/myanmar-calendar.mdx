## 📱 Overview

Myanmar Calendar shows every day in both calendars at once: the Western date, the Myanmar date and moon, Sabbath days, holidays and the traditional astrological days. Around that it adds what people actually plan with: events and class timetables, day notes and a merit diary, a holiday list with long-weekend planning and reminders, search that understands typed dates, home-screen widgets you can tick events off on, apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS, and **Calendar Studio** for designing a calendar to print or share.

The interface is in Myanmar and English, and calendar data is available in six languages (Myanmar, English, Zawgyi, Mon, Shan and Karen). Calculations come from my [`myanmar_calendar_dart`](https://pub.dev/packages/myanmar_calendar_dart) package, and several screens use the widgets from [`flutter_mmcalendar`](https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_mmcalendar).

## ✨ Features

### 🗓️ Home calendar

- Myanmar and Western dates in one month grid, with the moon, holidays, Sabbath and events on each day
- **22 grid layouts**, with **Almanac** as the default: lunar day, moon, holiday, events and a strip for Sabbath and good or careful days, plus a marker where a Myanmar month begins
- Layouts such as Myanmar months, Focus week, Auspice, Moon band, Hero today and Week ribbons
- **12 grid themes** (Pagoda, Jade, Lacquer, Thadingyut, Thingyan and more), each with its own palette, numerals and motif

### 🌙 Day details and astrology

- Myanmar date, Sasana year, Buddhist era and the Shan year
- Moon phase, with a countdown to the next full moon, new moon and Sabbath
- Astrology said the way people say it: ဥပုသ် နေ့, ရက်ရာဇာ, ပြဿဒါး နေ့, ဘင်္ဂဖွား, လူ နက္ခတ်, ဘဒြ နှစ်
- A Nagahle compass, and an AI prompt for a horoscope, fortune-telling or divination reading (nothing is sent from the app)
- **Good days**: the days with good astrological marks in the next 1, 3 or 6 months
- **Birth** tool: the birth sign, Mahabote, Nakhat, exact age and the next Western and Myanmar birthdays
- Share any day as an image, square or story size, in your theme and calendar language

### 🏮 Holidays

- Public, religious and cultural holidays, searchable and filterable, with reminders and **Good to know** for each festival: what happens, tips, etiquette and where to see it
- **Long weekends**: the breaks the year's holidays make, and the days of leave that make them longer ("Take Fri 20 Nov off → 5 days off")
- **Reminders**: Sabbath reminders the evening before or that morning, and a morning daily summary of the date, holidays and the day's marks
- Remote-config overrides to add or disable holidays without an app update

### 👀 Views

- Year, month, week and day
- **Myanmar month**, laid out by its waxing and waning fortnights
- **Agenda** of the coming holidays, Sabbath days, moon days and events

### 📌 Events

- **Weekly timetables**: several weekdays, every N weeks, an end date or count, and a time for each day, all in one save
- **Multiple dates**: one event on any set of days, picked on the Myanmar calendar
- Daily, monthly and yearly repeats, or **by Myanmar date** (every Tabaung full moon, a Myanmar birthday); change or skip one occurrence; categories, priorities, tags and reminders
- **Share an event** as a QR code, a link or an `.ics` file, Myanmar-date repeats included
- Every date picker takes a typed date, in English or Myanmar ("13 Apr 2027", "Tabaung full moon 1388", "next Sabbath")

### 📝 Notes, merit and search

- **Day notes** on any day, with a dot in the grid and a searchable list
- **Merit diary**: log donations, precepts and meditation, with a Sabbath streak and yearly totals
- **Search** for typed dates, holidays, events and notes

### 💾 Backup

- Back up events, notes, merit entries, custom holidays and reminder choices to one file, and restore by merging or replacing
- Export events as `.ics`, and import from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and other `.ics` files

### 🎨 Calendar Studio

- Design wall, desk, social and story calendars from a gallery of presets
- Edit text, images, shapes, decorations, calendar grids and holiday legends on the page
- Project themes, page and layer management, undo and redo
- Export to PDF, PNG, JPEG or ZIP at each page's own paper size, never stretched
- Save projects (`.mmcstudio`) and templates (`.mmctemplate`)

### 🔄 Converter

- Myanmar ↔ Western conversion, date difference, date arithmetic and a moon phase finder

### 📲 Widgets and personalisation

- iOS widgets (Today, Moon, Week, Countdown, Astrology day, Myanmar year, Today's note, Good days) and lock-screen widgets; Android Jetpack Glance widgets in five styles
- **Interactive widgets**: tick an event done right on the Agenda and Today widgets, and open the note editor from the Today's note widget
- Clear (iOS-like) and Material app styles, light and dark, theme presets, custom colours, alternate icons and **Easy reading** (larger text)
- English for Western dates, whatever the calendar language
- Onboarding with quick setup, and a What's new sheet after each update

## ⌚️ Watches

The same calendar on the wrist, built from the same Dart code as the phone's widgets.

- **Apple Watch**: today and the next days, Coming up, and watch-face complications. The iPhone app sends the watch half a year of days, so it keeps going without the phone.
- **Wear OS**: a standalone app with day pages, each day's astrology and Coming up, plus a Tile and a watch-face complication, in Myanmar, English, Shan, Mon or Karen. See [Myanmar Calendar for Wear OS](/projects/myanmar-calendar-wear).

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Prerequisites

- Flutter 3.x with Dart 3.11+
- Android Studio or VS Code with the Flutter tools
- Xcode for iOS builds (macOS)

### Run Locally

```bash
git clone https://github.com/mixin27/mmcalendar.git
cd mmcalendar

# The repository is a Flutter workspace
flutter pub get

cd apps/myanmar_calendar
flutter run
```

### Release Builds

```bash
cd apps/myanmar_calendar

flutter build appbundle --release   # Android
flutter build ipa --release         # iOS (macOS only)
flutter build web --release         # Web

cd ../myanmar_calendar_wear
flutter build appbundle --release   # Wear OS
```

## ☁️ Remote Config

### Holiday Overrides

- **Key**: `holidays_config_v2`
- **Type**: JSON string

```json
{
  "customHolidayRules": [
    {
      "id": "team_founding_day",
      "name": "Team Founding Day",
      "type": "other",
      "localizedNames": { "my": "အသင်းတည်ထောင်နေ့", "en": "Team Founding Day" },
      "rule": { "kind": "westernDate", "month": 9, "day": 1 }
    }
  ],
  "disabledHolidays": ["halloween"],
  "disabledHolidaysByYear": { "2027": ["valentinesDay"] },
  "disabledHolidaysByDate": { "2026-10-31": ["halloween"] }
}
```

Holiday IDs: [holiday_id.dart](https://github.com/mixin27/myanmar_calendar_dart/blob/main/lib/src/models/holiday_id.dart)

### App Updates (optional)

`app_update_enabled`, `app_update_latest_build_number`, `app_update_latest_version`, `app_update_min_supported_build_number`, the force and optional update titles and messages, `app_update_release_notes`, and the Android, iOS and web store URLs.

## 🏗️ Architecture

A Flutter workspace: one app, feature packages, and shared and integration layers that features reach only through ports.

```text
mmcalendar/
├── apps/
│   ├── myanmar_calendar/          # Routing, DI, native widgets, Apple Watch app
│   └── myanmar_calendar_wear/     # Wear OS app, Tile and complication
├── packages/
│   ├── features/                  # calendar, views, holidays, events, notes, calendar_studio,
│   │                              # converter, home_widgets, onboarding, whats_new, settings
│   ├── shared/                    # core, localizations, ui_kit, moon, motifs, widget_payload
│   └── integrations/              # database (Drift), firebase, app_update, telegram_web
├── site/                          # calendar.kyawzayartun.com (Next.js)
├── store/                         # Store screenshots and listing text
└── scripts/
```

### Stack

- Flutter and Dart, `flutter_bloc`, `go_router`, `drift`, `get_it` + `injectable`
- `myanmar_calendar_dart` for the calendar engine and `flutter_mmcalendar` for calendar widgets
- WidgetKit (iOS) and Jetpack Glance (Android) for home-screen widgets, with App Intents and Glance actions for ticking events off
- SwiftUI and WatchConnectivity for the Apple Watch app; Wear OS Tiles and complications in Kotlin
- One widget payload, built in Dart, that every widget, watch and Tile draws
- Firebase Remote Config, Analytics and Crashlytics, only with the user's consent
