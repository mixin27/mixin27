The Myanmar calendar on a Wear OS watch. It is the Wear OS form factor of [Myanmar Calendar](/projects/myanmar-calendar): the same Play listing, the same calendar engine and holiday catalogue. Since version 2.7.0 it lives in the [mmcalendar](https://github.com/mixin27/mmcalendar) monorepo next to the phone app. It needs no phone to run.

## ✨ Features

- **Day pages**: today and the next six days, one per screen. Swipe or turn the crown. Each shows the moon, "Waxing 5" or "Full Moon", the Myanmar month and year, the Western date, and Sabbath and holiday tags. On a plain day, today shows how far the next Sabbath is.
- **Astrology**: tap a day for its good and caution marks (Yatyaza, Pyathada, Thamanyo and the rest, by the phone's rule), Nagahle, Mahabote and Nakhat.
- **Coming up**: the next holidays, Sabbath days and full and new moons, with countdowns.
- **Tile**: today's Myanmar date, the moon phase and the next holiday or Sabbath.
- **Complication**: the moon and day of the fortnight, the Myanmar date, or the moon's illumination, for any watch face that takes them.
- **Five calendar languages**: Myanmar, English, Shan, Mon and Karen. The Tile and complication follow the app's language.
- **Ambient mode**: today's date, dimmed.

## 🏗️ How it works

- **One calendar everywhere.** Dates come from [`myanmar_calendar_dart`](https://pub.dev/packages/myanmar_calendar_dart), holidays from the phone app's catalogue, and astrology tones from the phone's good-day rule. Words come from the shared localizations package, so the watch never disagrees with the phone.
- **One payload.** The Tile and complication are native Kotlin, but they don't calculate anything. Dart writes a slim version of the payload the phone's home-screen widgets use (90 days of dates, the words and the Coming up list), and Kotlin only draws it. WorkManager rewrites it daily, and a midnight alarm refreshes the Tile and complication.
- **Small app.** No network permission, no Firebase and no state-management framework: the screens are plain widgets over a day model.

```text
apps/myanmar_calendar_wear/
├── lib/
│   ├── app/                 # App and colours (the phone's dark tokens)
│   ├── features/
│   │   ├── home/            # Day pages, Coming up, ambient day
│   │   ├── day_details/     # Day model and the astrology screen
│   │   └── settings/        # Calendar language
│   └── watch_payload/       # Writes and schedules the Tile/complication payload
└── android/app/src/main/kotlin/dev/mixin27/mmcalendar/watch/
                             # Tile, complication, midnight refresh
```

## 🚀 Build

```bash
git clone https://github.com/mixin27/mmcalendar.git
cd mmcalendar && flutter pub get

cd apps/myanmar_calendar_wear
flutter run                          # on a Wear OS emulator or watch
flutter build appbundle --release    # for Play, signed with the phone app's upload key
```

## 🕰️ History

The first version (January 2026) was a separate repository, [`myanmar_calendar_wear`](https://github.com/mixin27/myanmar_calendar_wear), with its own holiday logic and no Tile. Version 2.7.0 moved it into the monorepo, switched it to the phone's calendar code, added the Tile and complication, and redesigned the screens to match the Apple Watch app.
